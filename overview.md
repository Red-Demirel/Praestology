  – R2 Structure (distinctions emerge)  
  – R3 Motion (tension drives change)  
  – R4 Progress (every resolution creates new differences)

  Primary: `core/specifications.yaml`, `manifesto.md`, `Praestology short.md`.

  Primary: `core/derivative-reality-clarification.yaml`, `core/process-to-observable-hierarchy.yaml`, `core/specifications.yaml`.

  Primary: `core/closure-configuration-ratio.yaml`, related constant and quanta files.
