* **The Paradigm Shift:** Rejects the legacy "time-as-dimension" parameter ($t \in \mathbb{R}$) and the static Minkowski/STA background. Time is redefined as the internal, sequential transformational order ($n$) of the algebra's own operations ($X \mapsto R X R^\dagger$).

#### Part 3: From "Is Not Not" to Matter
